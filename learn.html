Quick definitions

Issuer

The entity presenting or structuring an asset opportunity.

Holder

A participant represented as owning or accessing an asset position.

Funding progress

The UI percentage showing how much of an offering has been allocated.

Data Room

Organized legal, financial, risk and offering documents.

Settlement

The system process that finalizes payment and ownership transfer.
